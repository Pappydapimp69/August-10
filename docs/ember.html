Ember

Click anywhere to start a fire — or just watch the chronicle. Three tribes gather wood and quarry stone, raise camps into walled halls, forge spears, and build shrines to the Moon, the Storm, or the Grove. They send gift caravans, demand tribute, swear friendships, nurse permanent grudges, and face dilemmas their own character decides — mercy or hoarding, honor or spoils. Wars still happen. And everything that rages here — fire and war alike — carries a finite, dwindling budget. Nothing rages forever, by construction.

Everything here is a recorded lesson wearing a costume. Fire and war carry generation budgets (the-answering-deep#E11; tension T24 records that a cautious opponent stalemates forever without one). Diplomacy is earned, not declared — gift caravans and paid tribute move relations the way an earned currency moves a negotiation (barter-economy / earned-currency) — and every war leaves a permanent grudge scoped to that pair, capping how warm the friendship can ever become again (non-decaying-pivotal-events / traumatic-memories-dont-fade). Each tribe's character is two seeded values, mercy and honor, and the moral dilemmas — feed a starving neighbor or hoard, shelter enemy wounded or drive them out, return spoils taken after a truce or keep them — are resolved by those values and written to the chronicle (trust-as-character). Rituals ride the sky's own clock: the Moon tribe worships at night, the Storm tribe in the rain, the Grove at first light (day-night / clock-as-pressure). Defenders chase only inside their ring and reset (pursuit-ring-territory), a spotted raider commits the whole tribe (coupled-enemy-alert), mood arbitration is tiered because a margin inside the urgent band can defeat a veto (hysteresis-can-defeat-a-saturated-veto), and defenders out-accelerate raiders because speed parity decides chases. Spears are forged from wood and stone, carried to battle, and consumed by it. The synthesized sound is gesture-gated, fire-and-forget, guarded, clock-scheduled, and voice-budgeted (dog#E1, shadow#E1, dog#E6). One seed, no ambient entropy: world 7 fights the same wars, swears the same friendships, and forgives none of the same grudges, on every machine, forever.
