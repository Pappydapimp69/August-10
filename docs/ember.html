Ember

Click anywhere to start a fire — or just watch the chronicle. Three tribes forage wood, stone and food, grow their population, raise camps into walled halls, research a small tech tree, and go to war — their territory ring widening as they grow. A single tree gives a fixed number of harvests, then it's gone: it almost always regrows a few pixels away after a while, but sometimes dies for good and salts the ground around it. Building a structure, growing the population, and researching a technology all cost real resources. Nothing here — fire, war, or a single tree — can rage or grow forever, by construction.

Everything here is a recorded lesson wearing a costume, plus one system built without a precedent to lean on. Fire and war carry generation budgets (the-answering-deep#E11; tension T24 records that a cautious opponent stalemates forever without one). The territory ring widens with population on a square-root curve — the same shape that keeps a difficulty ramp from outrunning the player (sublinear-difficulty-ramp / sqrt-curve-to-2x), so ten more mouths to feed roughly doubles the ring, not the population. Diplomacy is earned, not declared (barter-economy / earned-currency), and every war leaves a permanent grudge capping how warm a friendship can become again (non-decaying-pivotal-events). Each tribe's mercy and honor resolve its moral dilemmas and are written to the chronicle (trust-as-character). Rituals ride the sky's clock (day-night / clock-as-pressure); defenders chase only inside their ring and reset (pursuit-ring-territory); a spotted raider commits the tribe (coupled-enemy-alert); mood arbitration stays tiered because a margin inside the urgent band can defeat a veto (hysteresis-can-defeat-a-saturated-veto). The tree lifecycle has no recorded kernel behind it — it was designed, not retrieved: a harvested tree yields a fixed number of loads, then is consumed; after a delay it rolls once — 5% salts the ground into a dead stump that blocks any new tree within 2px forever, 95% a sapling takes root 3–5px away in a random direction. Building a level, growing the population, and researching a technology all deduct real resources on the spot, the same discipline the shrine and armory already used. The synthesized sound is gesture-gated, fire-and-forget, guarded, clock-scheduled, voice-budgeted (dog#E1, shadow#E1, dog#E6). One seed, no ambient entropy: world 7 grows the same forest, the same tribes, and forgives none of the same grudges, on every machine, forever.
