Ember

Click anywhere to start a fire — or just watch. Three tribes gather wood, raise their camps from firepits into walled halls, envy each other's stockpiles, and go to war: raiders slip out to steal from enemy camps, defenders chase them off inside their own ring of territory. Storms still roll in, lightning still strikes, flowers still get eaten. And everything that rages here — fire and war alike — carries a finite, dwindling budget. Nothing can rage forever, by construction.

Everything here is a recorded lesson wearing a costume. Fire — and now war — carries a generation budget: a radius-capped fire was once observed relighting the same cells forever (the-answering-deep#E11), and a cautious opponent-AI was once observed stalemating indefinitely (tension T24) — so every war opens with a finite raid budget and must end; a truce with its own timer follows, which is hysteresis applied to diplomacy. Defenders chase intruders only inside a ring around their own camp, then break off and walk home (pursuit-ring-territory / defend-and-reset), and one defender spotting a raider is what commits the chase — the harmless sensor triggers the hunter (coupled-enemy-alert). Mood arbitration is tiered because a switching margin inside the urgent band can defeat a veto (hysteresis-can-defeat-a-saturated-veto): fleeing fire outranks war, war outranks chores, and the margin governs only the chores. Defenders accelerate harder than raiders because movement-speed parity, not cleverness, decides a chase (movement-speed-parity-dominates-flee-ai). Trees are spaced by min-distance rejection (sandbox-scatter#E1); the synthesized sound initializes on your click, fire-and-forget, every call guarded (dog#E1), scheduled on the AudioContext clock (shadow#E1) under a voice budget (dog#E6). Weather, worldgen, wander targets, and every battle roll come from one seed with no ambient entropy: world 7 fights the same wars on every machine, forever.
